Every swap posts a refundable bond. Fifteen seconds later the pool checks what the price actually did. Benign flow gets it back. Informed flow pays for the harm it caused.

The problem

Liquidity providers lose to flow they cannot see

CEX-DEX arbitrage takes the good side of every quote and liquidity providers wear the difference. That is loss-versus-rebalancing — measured at $233.8M over nineteen months on mainnet, roughly three quarters of it captured by three searchers.

It is the largest unsolved cost of providing liquidity onchain, and it is not a bug in any one pool. It is what happens when a price is quoted to someone who knows more than the quote does.

02Hindsight
Why the existing defenses do not close it

Every one of them guesses

A static fee

Charges retail and arbitrage the same number. Raise it and you throttle the flow you want; lower it and you subsidise the flow you don't.

A dynamic fee

Reads volatility — but volatility is not intent. It cannot tell a retail swap from a bot, so it punishes everyone in the same conditions equally.

A priority-fee / MEV tax

Prices the intent to be first. On a fast rollup searchers stop bidding for priority and spam cheap reverting probes instead — and a reverted probe pays zero.

All three price flow before they can possibly know what it was.

03Hindsight
The turn

The one signal that cannot be faked is what the price actually did

Markout — the distance the price kept travelling in your direction after you left — cannot be spoofed, cannot be revert-spammed, and does not need to be predicted. It only needs to be measured, which means waiting.

So Hindsight waits. The trade executes immediately; the price of that trade is settled fifteen seconds later from evidence that did not exist when it landed.

θ scales with volatility, so a violent tape does not confiscate honest flow — and it is computed from the two minutes before your swap landed, then frozen into the swap record at execution. You cannot move the bar you are judged against.

We did not get this right first time. Through v6, θ shared a window with the markout it judged: on the real tape 927 swaps were acquitted by their own companion prints. Under a window that closes before the trade lands that number is zero — asserted as an equality, not a bound.

08Hindsight
Three adversarial audit rounds · every finding has a repro committed before its fix

What we got wrong

Was — out-of-sample correlation 0.441
Now — ≈ 0, published as a negative result

The label was anchored at the swap's own execution price, making it identically markout + later drift. 91% of that number was the tautology the test existed to rebut, and a placebo tape with no predictability scored higher.

Was — "0.0% of revenue from benign flow"
Now — 5.6%, with the tautology labelled

A string literal. True by construction, because we defined benign as the swaps we did not charge. Against an independent label the honest figure is 5.6%, against 14.4% for a flat fee.

Was — "a JIT captures at most ~15% of the pot"
Now — the bound is gone; residency replaces it

Our own test measured a single epoch and never repeated the snipe, which was the entire attack. Repeated across twelve flushes a JIT took 98%. Liquidity must now sit 60s before withdrawal, so the snipe cannot be atomic.

A number you can check is worth more than a number that flatters us. All three retractions ship on the site, in place, beside their corrected figures.
